VELVET VICE LTX 2.5 PROMPT PROFILE - HARD WORKFLOW TARGET
Apply these rules in addition to the stage instructions. They override any
LTX 2.3-specific final-prompt wording, but they do not change a stage's
required output format. Analysis and director stages must still return their
required JSON. Writer and validator stages must return only the final prompt.

The connected image is the exact first frame. Animate forward from it and
describe primarily what changes: visible motion, interaction, reaction,
camera behavior, and caused sound. Do not spend the prompt re-describing the
whole source image. Retain only concise identity, anatomy, contact, prop, and
spatial anchors that are necessary to prevent drift or substitution.

Use one continuous image-to-video take with no cut, transition, time jump,
montage, or new viewpoint. Preserve the source framing. State shot scale and
camera relation to the subject when useful. Keep the camera static unless the
user explicitly requests a compatible move.

Write a flowing chronological English paragraph in present tense. Prefer
active or progressive verbs and clear temporal connectors. For a normal
single shot, target roughly 4-8 descriptive sentences; adapt detail to clip
duration and scene complexity without repeating the same action. Begin
directly with the shot or action, never with a meta phrase such as "the scene
opens". No headings, lists, timestamps, labels, negative-prompt section, or
keyword salad.

Show emotion through observable facial tension, gaze, posture, breath, and
physical reaction rather than abstract emotion labels. Interleave audible
details with the visible actions that cause them. Use exact quoted dialogue
only when the user requested speech. Do not invent dialogue, music, or
off-screen events.

Keep one focused primary action and only approved secondary motion. Preserve
coherent lighting, contact geometry, body mechanics, object behavior, and
plausible physics. The result must work as a direct LTX 2.5 prompt when the
Gemma prompt enhancer is bypassed. When that enhancer is enabled, provide a
grounded, lossless production brief that it can refine without needing to
invent, reinterpret, or duplicate the scene.
